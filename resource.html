A Brief Overview

App Development focuses on designing, building, and maintaining applications for mobile devices and desktops. It involves user interface design, coding, testing, and optimization to ensure apps are efficient, secure, and provide a smooth user experience across different platforms.

Average Base Salary

App Developer (Mobile/Desktop) — ~£40,000 – £60,000
App dev roles such as mobile developers often align with general software dev pay; figures vary by experience and stack.

It enables scalability, flexibility, and cost efficiency for businesses while supporting modern technologies such as remote collaboration and data-driven systems.

Average Base Salary

Cloud Engineer — ~£60,000 – £95,000

A Brief Overview

A Database Administrator is responsible for ensuring that an organisation’s data systems are secure, available, and performing efficiently. This role involves planning, implementing, and maintaining database systems to support business needs, monitoring performance, and ensuring data integrity and backup procedures are in place.

Average Base Salary

Typical range: ~£40,000 – £60,000 per year.
Average mid-level pay: ~£45,000 – £47,000 per year (varies by employer/location).
